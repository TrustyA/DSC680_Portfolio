Traction vs Drivetrain Safety Analysis
Overview

This project investigates the relationship between vehicle drivetrain configurations and safety outcomes while examining the role of traction-related factors.

Objective

The objective was to determine whether drivetrain type alone contributes significantly to vehicle safety or whether other factors play a larger role.

Data

Data from multiple automotive safety and vehicle information sources were combined for analysis.

Tools & Technologies
Python
Pandas
SQLite
Matplotlib
Seaborn
Methodology

Datasets were merged, cleaned, and analyzed to compare safety outcomes across different drivetrain configurations and vehicle characteristics.

Results

The findings suggest that traction, vehicle design, and driver behavior often have a greater influence on safety outcomes than drivetrain type alone.
