Medical Cost Analysis
Overview

This project analyzes medical insurance costs and the factors that influence healthcare expenses. The analysis focuses on identifying relationships between demographic characteristics and insurance charges.

Objective

The objective was to determine which variables have the strongest impact on medical costs and to develop a better understanding of healthcare spending patterns.

Data

The dataset contains information such as age, sex, BMI, smoking status, number of children, geographic region, and insurance charges.

Tools & Technologies
Python
Pandas
NumPy
Matplotlib
Seaborn
Jupyter Notebook
Methodology

Exploratory data analysis was performed to examine relationships between variables and insurance costs. Visualizations and statistical analysis were used to identify key drivers of medical expenses.

Results

The analysis showed that smoking status and age were among the strongest predictors of insurance costs, with significant differences observed across demographic groups.
